# W09-A — Relay onboarding with durable leases

## Objective

Claim bounded delivery batches under durable leases, publish through a transport port, acknowledge only after a successful publish, and recover expired leases or quarantine bounded failures.

## Authority and inputs

- [W-9 requirements](../../../work-items.md#w-9--relay-onboarding-and-expose-the-repair-snapshot)
- [Persistence and transaction model](../../../feature-implementation-plan.md#persistence-and-transaction-model)
- [D-9](../../../artifacts/implementation-decision-log.md)
- V2 `claim_outbox_deliveries`, `acknowledge_outbox_delivery`, `quarantine_outbox_delivery`, `append_audit_fact`.

## Outputs and ownership

Own the relay worker, the transport port, lease configuration and focused tests. Do not expose the snapshot.

## Acceptance

- Claim is bounded (limit and lease ranges) and concurrency safe (`skip locked`).
- Publish happens outside the state transaction; acknowledgement follows success only.
- A crash between publish and acknowledgement leaves the delivery leased and it is redelivered after lease expiry.
- Retry attempts are bounded; exhausted or non-retryable failures quarantine with a fixed reason.
- Restart preserves the immutable event and resumes delivery.
