## Composition

`FirstActivationTransaction` runs subject resolution, prerequisite evaluation/activation and the
onboarding intent inside one `TransactionRunner` transaction. `ActivationApplicationConfiguration`
wires the activation repository/service and the composer; `MessagingApplicationConfiguration` owns the
intent writer so cross-module persistence adapters stay inside their module (architecture rule).

## End-to-end proof (`FirstActivationIntegrationTest`, 4 tests)

| Scenario | Observed |
