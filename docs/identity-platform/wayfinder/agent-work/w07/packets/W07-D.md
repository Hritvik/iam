# W07-D — Close rate-limit, correlation, and diagnostic privacy boundaries

## Objective

Make the public token boundary resistant to quota exhaustion and ensure every response and diagnostic remains correlated and free of credentials, proofs, nonces, tokens, and global identity.

## Authority and inputs

- [W-7 requirements](../../../work-items.md#W-7--enforce-native-dpop-and-durable-replay-protection)
- [W-7 decisions](../../../artifacts/work-item-07-decisions.md)
- Audit findings on client-only rate limits, missing error correlation IDs, and Jackson serialization of record accessors.

## Outputs and ownership

Produce a limiter request carrying protected client, trusted network bucket, and challenge/retry category; consistent correlation handling; and serialization-safe diagnostic types. Own only declared paths.

## Acceptance

- Challenge and retry have separate budgets keyed by client plus trusted network bucket.
- Local development limiter has bounded key cardinality; production-like profiles require the external capability.
- Every success, OAuth error, and nonce challenge echoes a valid supplied correlation ID or generates one.
- Jackson serialization and `toString()` of D-owned command, outcome, authentication, and error types reveal none of the seeded code, verifier, proof, nonce, token, or global identity canaries. A/B own privacy for their value types; W07-I owns the cross-packet diagnostic canary.
- Captured configured logs and metric identifiers/tags remain clean; absent trace/persistence surfaces are described accurately.
- Response and error shapes remain contract-closed.
- Converter coverage includes duplicate and unknown parameters, wrong grant or content type, query-confused form input, anonymous or secret client attempts, and excluded OAuth/OIDC endpoints. W07-E owns ordering that places limiting before expensive orchestration.
