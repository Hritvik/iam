## Acceptance

- Header, claims, JWK, algorithm, curve, signature, and member sets remain closed.
- `jti` has at least 22 allowed characters in code and contract.
- Padded or noncanonical compact segments and high-S ES256 signatures fail.
- Token target equals the migration-owned issuer plus `/oauth2/token` after one canonical interpretation.
- Dot segments, ambiguous ports, fragments, userinfo, and noncanonical percent encoding fail.
- Existing token-proof/resource-proof `ath`, nonce, thumbprint, time, and method semantics remain green.
